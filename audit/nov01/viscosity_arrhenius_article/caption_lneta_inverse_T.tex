\caption{STACIE viscosity as a function of composition at 1873~K (a) and inverse temperature for pure Ti on a logarithmic viscosity scale (b). Colored symbols show PBE and \rscan{} estimates. In (a), lines connect sampled states; in (b), solid colored lines show weighted Arrhenius fits, $\ln[\eta/(\mathrm{mPa\,s})]=a+B/T$, fitted with weights $(\eta/\sigma_\eta)^2$. The composition data use 20~ps production trajectories. In (b), the PBE values at 1873 and 2000~K combine archived 20~ps trajectories with two 20~ps segments from each new 40~ps trajectory, using a common spectral cutoff of 1.25~THz; the 2100~K value uses full 40~ps trajectories. These pooled estimates remain exploratory because preparation differs and spectral diagnostics are not uniformly satisfied. Error bars in (a) are formal estimator standard errors; those in (b) are the transformed endpoints $\ln[(\eta\pm\sigma_\eta)/(\mathrm{mPa\,s})]$. They exclude systematic uncertainties; data provenance is retained in the supporting records. Black squares in (a) are the experimental correlations of Takeda \textit{et al.}\cite{Takeda2025}; open squares require a 3~K extrapolation. The dashed curve with an asterisk in (b) is a provisional transcription of the Ishikawa \textit{et al.}\cite{Ishikawa2012} correlation; shading denotes its reported 10.3\% uncertainty, not a specified confidence level.}
