\caption{STACIE viscosity as a function of composition at 1873~K (a) and temperature for pure Ti (b). Colored symbols show PBE and \rscan{} estimates; lines connect sampled states. The composition data use 20~ps production trajectories. In (b), the PBE values at 1873 and 2000~K combine archived 20~ps trajectories with two 20~ps segments from each new 40~ps trajectory, using a common spectral cutoff of 1.25~THz; the 2100~K value uses full 40~ps trajectories. The additional PBE value at 1941~K is an archived 20~ps estimate from an earlier parameter set and is included for comparison. These pooled estimates remain exploratory because preparation differs and spectral diagnostics are not uniformly satisfied. Error bars are formal estimator standard errors and exclude systematic uncertainties; data provenance is retained in the supporting records. Black squares in (a) are the experimental correlations of Takeda \textit{et al.}\cite{Takeda2025}; open squares require a 3~K extrapolation. The dashed curve with an asterisk in (b) is a provisional transcription of the Ishikawa \textit{et al.}\cite{Ishikawa2012} correlation; shading denotes its reported 10.3\% uncertainty, not a specified confidence level.}
